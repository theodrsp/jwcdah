Easy Homemade Fried Rice

A quick and delicious dish for a weeknight dinner

Ingredient

2 Cups of cold cooked rice, 2 cloves of garlic, and 1 tabletspoon of soy sauce.

Cooking Steps

Step 1: Preparation

Chop the garlic finely and heat the oil in a pan

Step 2: Cooking

Safety Tip

Be Careful when adding rice to hot oil to avoid splashing

Chef's Secret Note

Using day-old rice prevent it from getting mushy
